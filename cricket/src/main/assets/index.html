How to bat
Aim: push the joystick where you want the ball to go on screen. Down sends it straight back past the bowler, left and right go square, up goes behind (late cut or a ramp over the keeper). The aim stays until you change it. Tap the middle of the stick to block.

Ground or Air: GROUND is safer (fours and runs). AIR goes for six but can be caught.

HIT when the ball reaches the bat. The timing guide helps: a yellow spot shows where the ball will bounce, you hear a soft thud when it bounces, and a white ring closes onto the bat. Press HIT as the ring closes (it turns green in the perfect window). The meter after each ball shows how early or late you were. You can switch the guide off in the menu.

Swipe: for quick play, swipe on the pitch in the direction you want: it aims and hits in one move.

Read the ball: short balls: pull or cut (left / right). Full balls: drive (down / down-left). On the pads: flick it to the leg side. Yorkers: block or drive straight along the ground.

Field map (bottom-left): white dots are ring fielders, gold dots are deep. The field changes every over.

⚡ Power: perfect hits fill it; when full your next shot is a power shot. 🔥 On fire: back-to-back boundaries make timing easier. Free hit after a no-ball: you can't be out.
