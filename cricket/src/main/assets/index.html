How to play
1. Aim. Press an arrow to pick where to hit. Press two arrows together (or one quickly after the other) for diagonals: ▲+▶ is a cover drive, ▲+◀ a slog over midwicket, ▼+▶ a late cut, ▼ alone a ramp over the keeper. The middle button is Block.

2. Ground or Air. GROUND is safer: fours, singles and twos. AIR goes for six but can be caught.

3. HIT when the ball reaches the bat (you can also tap the pitch). Perfect timing hits hardest.

4. Read the ball. Short balls: cut ▶ or pull ◀. Full balls: drive ▲. On your pads: glance ◀▼ or flick ◀. Yorkers: just ▲ along the ground.

5. Find the gaps. The field map shows where the fielders stand. They move every over.

⚡ Power: perfect timing fills the meter. When full, your next shot is a power shot.
🔥 On fire: back-to-back boundaries make timing easier.
Free hit: after a no-ball you can't be caught or bowled. Swing big!
