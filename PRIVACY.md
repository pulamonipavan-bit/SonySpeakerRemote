# Privacy Policy: Talk with Zippy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Talk with Zippy is a speaking-practice game for young children (ages 5 and under), meant to be played together with a parent or carer. We built it to collect as little as possible.

## What the app does NOT do
- No ads, no in-app purchases and no links that leave the app.
- No accounts, names, photos, contacts, location or device identifiers are collected.
- No analytics or tracking tools are included.
- The app has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.
- It does **not** save recordings of your child's voice.

## Microphone
The app asks for microphone permission so that it can hear your child's answers and cheer them on.
- To understand words, the app uses the **speech service built into your phone**. It tries the phone's **on-device** speech recognition first, where the sound stays on the phone.
- If on-device recognition isn't available, your phone's speech provider (for example, Google's speech services on most Android phones) may process the audio to turn it into words. That processing is governed by the provider's own privacy policy (for Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only receives the recognised words. It does not receive or keep the audio.
- In the "Copy me" game the app records a few seconds of sound into the phone's memory, plays it back in a funny voice, then discards it. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

## Information stored on the phone
So that grown-ups can see progress, the app keeps a small amount of information **only on this phone**:
- which practice words the child said, and how often
- today's play time and the daily time limit
- the gifts collected in the game
- settings such as voice, sound level and helper lines

This information never leaves the phone. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Talk with Zippy → Storage → Clear storage.

## Permissions used
- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** to hear the child's answers, as described above.
- **Vibrate:** for gentle tap feedback.

## Children
The app is designed for young children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Grown-up settings are behind a simple adult check.
