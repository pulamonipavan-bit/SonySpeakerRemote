# Google Play store listing: Talk with Zippy

## App name (max 30 characters)
Talk with Zippy: First Words

## Short description (max 80 characters)
A gentle superhero game that helps toddlers say their first English words.

## Full description
Help your little one start talking. Zippy the superhero shows a picture, says the word, and waits for your child to try. When they say it, Zippy cheers with a stunt and a surprise gift.

**Made for ages 2 to 4, to play together with a grown-up**
• Say it: everyday words like dog, ball, roti, auto and juice, with lively 3D pictures that run, hop and fly
• Tell Zippy: your child says "jump!" or "spin!" and Zippy does it
• Finish the rhyme: Twinkle, twinkle, little… star!
• Two words: "more milk", "big ball", modelled as full sentences
• Copy me: Zippy repeats your child in a funny voice

**Built on how children learn to talk**
• Short, clear models and patient waiting
• Gentle help when your child is quiet: a choice ("a dog, or a cat?"), then a sound cue
• Zippy adds one more word ("Dog! A happy dog!") to grow phrases
• A small set of "today's words" repeats all day, because toddlers learn by repetition
• Understands toddler speech such as "tat" for cat and "nana" for banana

**Calm and safe**
• Daily time limit with a gentle "rest time" screen
• No ads, no purchases, no accounts, no internet access
• Grown-up area behind an adult check: see words said, choose the voice, adjust sounds
• Helper tips for parents during play

Talk with Zippy supports, but does not replace, a speech-language therapist. If you are worried about your child's speech, please speak to a professional.
